  Plus passive income: ~100–300 per message, gated by a 60s per-user cooldown
  (uncapped daily), and rare server-wide "drop" pickups (~1k–5k, luck-based).

  For scale: cheapest shop items (hereshoutout, color roles) are 400k–600k.

  Daily totals by playstyle

  - Casual (logs in once, does daily, spin, maybe one of hug/music, sends a
    normal amount of messages): ~20,000–25,000/day
  - Medium (checks in ~6–8 times across the day, hits whatever's off cooldown
    each time, chats a fair amount): ~200,000–250,000/day
  - Max/grinder (hits every cooldown the instant it's up, all day): ~3.5–3.7 
    million/day — enough to buy the entire shop catalog in a single day.

  The problem

  Per-hour value is inverted. The five-minute/ten-minute filler commands (paint,
  bake, draw, gfx) pay 20–100x more per hour than the commands designed to be
  rare/special (stargaze, event, even daily). A player should be rewarded more
  for waiting a week or a month, not less — right now stargaze (7–30 day
  cooldown) is the worst value per hour in the entire economy.

  Recommend nerfing (ranked by severity)

  1. paint — 45k/hr from a 5-min cooldown is the single worst offender. Cooldown
     → 30–60 min, or cut payout ~75%.
  2. bake — flat, risk-free 3,000 every 5 min. Cooldown → 20–30 min, or payout →
     ~500–800.
  3. draw — flat 5,000 every 10 min. Cooldown → 30–45 min, or payout →
     ~1,000–1,500.
  4. gfx — still well above baseline. Cooldown → 45–60 min or reduce payout.
  5. hug — least severe, lower priority.

  Recommend buffing

  1. stargaze — rarest command in the game (weekly for boosters, monthly
     otherwise), yet pays barely more than 2 dailies. Push to 60,000–100,000+,
     or add a wait-scaling bonus.
  2. event — 14-day cooldown for a modest payout. Roughly double it.
  3. daily — currently earns less per hour than five different low-effort filler
     commands, which undermines its role as the anchor reward. Consider
     12,000–15,000.

  Also flag

  buypaint is tagged as an econ-earning command but is actually a pure cost sink
  (subtracts 3k–4k) with zero mechanical link to paint — nothing checks whether
  you've bought supplies before painting. Looks like an unfinished feature.
  Either wire it in as an actual gate for paint (which would help fix paint's
  imbalance too) or drop it from !econ.
